# Niko Deskpet

Niko Deskpet is an AI-powered desktop pet that you can chat with.

Niko can move around your screen or you can make him move!

Here are the controls:

- Left-click on Niko to make him move toward your mouse cursor.

- Right-click on Niko to open the prompt window, where you can type a message. Press Enter to send it, and wait for his response!

- Moving the mouse wheel allows you to scroll the textbox (up or down).

In addition to chatting with you, Niko may occasionally move to a different spot on your screen (or you can ask him to move!).

NOTICE: Niko does not read information from your PC; it only reads its current position and the size of your screen.
